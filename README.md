A SillyTavern Preset where the LLM acts as the Game/Dungeon Master, and you are the Player. The main "engines" are made up of 4 parts: 

→ Resolution Engine: Identifies player actions, rolls dice, and applies mechanics 

→ Relationship Engine: Determines how NPCs feel about you, based on your actions. Basically, you can no longer talk your way into a character's pants. 

→ Chaos Engine: A system that can introduce contextually-relevant random events into roleplay.

→ Proactivity Engine: A system that makes it possible for NPCs to be a little proactive, that is, they won't just react to you, but will occasionally act on their own, too.
